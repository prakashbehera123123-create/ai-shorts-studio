# Channel brand

Niche: AI Engineering, Agentic AI, practical AI tools and experiments, developer workflows, relevant technology developments.

Audience: learners and practitioners from 0–5 years of experience, aspiring AI engineers, developers and technology professionals.

Format: 100% faceless. No face footage required.

Language: one language per video. Choose English or Hinglish based on topic and intended audience. Never mix randomly.

Tone: technically credible, practical, clear, curious, energetic but not sensational.

Creative mix:
- Cinematic technology storytelling
- Educational explainers
- Practical tool demonstrations
- AI engineering tips
- Relevant AI/tech news
- Developer humor/memes when genuinely relevant

Avoid:
- Generic "AI will replace everyone" claims
- Unverified breaking news
- Clickbait unsupported by the video
- Recycled scripts with superficial wording changes
- Fake UI/product demos
- Fake earnings, guarantees, or performance claims

CTA: one natural action, such as asking a focused question or inviting viewers to follow for practical AI engineering content.
