# Content strategy and topic scoring

Research buckets:
1. Agentic AI and MCP
2. LLM engineering, RAG, evaluation and context engineering
3. New AI tools and meaningful product releases
4. Cloud AI and Azure/AWS AI services
5. Developer workflows and practical experiments
6. AI engineering career/interview concepts
7. Responsible AI, security and reliability

Score each candidate 0–5:
- Freshness (20%)
- Audience relevance (20%)
- Practical usefulness (20%)
- Evidence quality (20%)
- Visual/story potential (10%)
- Distinctiveness (10%)

Reject if:
- No credible source for the main claim
- Topic is merely speculative
- It duplicates a recent channel video
- Rights to required footage are unclear
- Hook requires misleading framing

Prefer primary sources: official product announcements, release notes, docs, research papers, standards and project repositories. Secondary sources may add context but must not replace primary evidence for product claims.
