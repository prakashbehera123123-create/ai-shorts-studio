# Research topics
1. Read `config/channel-brand.md` and `config/content-strategy.md`.
2. Determine current date and research window. Default: last 7 days for news; evergreen topics may be included but labelled evergreen.
3. Ask research-analyst for 8–12 candidates using available search/browser tools.
4. Deduplicate against `content/topic-backlog.md` and recent production folders.
5. Score using the content-strategy weights. Reject low-evidence candidates.
6. Select up to 2 distinct topics. Do not force two if quality is weak.
7. Save each brief under `production/YYYY-MM-DD-topic-slug/brief.md` and sources under `sources.md`.
8. Update `content/topic-backlog.md`.
9. Return concise shortlist, scores, citations, and any research limitations.
Do not download footage or produce videos in this skill.
