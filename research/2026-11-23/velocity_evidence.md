# Shorts view-velocity evidence — 2026-11-23

Method (owner request 2026-11-23: rank by where niche Shorts get the most views fast and are liked most).
- YouTube `robots.txt` (read 2026-11-23) **disallows `/results`**, so search pages sorted by view count were NOT used.
  Only allowed paths were read in the built-in browser, by hand and rate-limited (~55 page loads, 2–4 s apart):
  `/hashtag/<tag>/shorts`, `/@channel/shorts`, `/channel/<id>/shorts`, and individual `/shorts/<id>` pages.
- No login, no API, no Studio, no downloads. Views come from the listing tile (rounded by YouTube). Upload date and likes
  come from the Short's page (`publishDate`, like-button label). Likes show as "hidden" where the creator hid them.
- views/day = lifetime views ÷ days since upload. This is a lifetime average, not a recent rate, and new uploads look faster.
- Hashtags sampled: #aiagents #aitools #cybersecurity #systemdesign #generativeai #machinelearning #agenticai #llm #chatgpt #aiinhindi.
  Channels sampled: ByteByteGo, IBM Technology, Fireship, Krish Naik, Chai aur Code, TechAkshay 2.0, Swastik.
- All accessed 2026-11-23.

## Patterns (niche only)

1. **"AI fails a simple question" (Hinglish)**: by far the fastest niche pattern, at 77K–280K views/day, repeated across
   one channel's whole catalogue. Those Shorts only show the failure. None of them explains *why*, which is our gap.
2. **Everyday-security fear-hooks** (selfie fingerprint ~206K/day; free Wi-Fi ~30K/day): very high likes per view.
   A myth-busting or "real risk" angle is open.
3. **Agent capability stories** ("agent built a browser in 7 days", ~21K/day, 22K likes): concrete outcome plus a number.
4. **Concept comparisons** (chatbot vs automation vs agent, ~17K/day).
5. **Hinglish research and tech-news explainers** (Swastik, Chai aur Code): 5–12K/day, a steady mid-tier.
6. **Evergreen "why is X so fast" system design** (ByteByteGo): huge lifetime totals but slow velocity now (<1K/day).

Caveats: this is a small, hand-collected sample that hashtag ranking biases toward popular items. It is not a statistical
measurement. Likes are hidden on the fastest Hinglish channel.
